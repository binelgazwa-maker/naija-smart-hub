🎯 How to study more effectively

Choose one clear topic, study in short focused sessions, take a brief break, then test yourself without looking at your notes. Return to difficult topics later.

💡 Build a simple daily learning habit

Set a realistic daily goal, remove distractions, keep track of what you learn and review your progress each week. Consistency is more useful than a huge plan you cannot maintain.

🔐 Stay safer online

Use strong, unique passwords, turn on two-step verification when available, avoid suspicious links and never share passwords or verification codes with anyone.

🧮 A quick percentage example

To find 10% of 500, calculate 500 × 10 ÷ 100 = 50. For any percentage, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.
